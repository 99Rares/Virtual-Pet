Each paid skin has idle1–3, hungry1–2, dance1–3, feed1–2, wake1–10 and preview, prefixed with its ID and an underscore. Classic keeps idle1–3, hungry1–2, dance1–3, feed1–2 and wake1–wake10, plus classic_preview. All share a 600×650 transparent canvas and matching pose registration.

The six paid sets contain 126 resources including previews, built by editing the corresponding approved classic poses. Feet share the y=590 baseline on a 600×650 canvas. Skins are wired into the app (shop Skins tab, `domain/Skins.kt`). Review sheets and eight-room composites are in art-review/.

Pupsy and Kira use the selected idle masters from the user's pet references. Kira keeps the original cute design chosen on 2026-11-07; the later forehead studies are alternatives. Both personal pets use the existing 20-frame animation contract plus a preview, at 600×650 with foot contact y=590. Their shop prices extend the existing progression at 140 and 160 coins.
